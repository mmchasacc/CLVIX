# CRM Training Simulator Platform

An AI-powered CRM onboarding and operational training simulator for telecom, customer service, retail, and sales organizations.

---

## Overview

This project aims to modernize onboarding and operational training for industries that rely on complex CRM systems and high-pressure customer interactions.

Instead of training employees directly in production environments, this platform provides a realistic simulation environment where users can safely practice workflows, solve customer cases, and improve operational confidence without risking real customer data or live accounts.

The platform is inspired by operational workflows commonly found in:

* telecom operators
* customer support centers
* retail telecom stores
* sales organizations
* outsourced call centers

However, the system uses:

* original UI/UX
* generated data
* abstracted workflows
* simulated environments

to avoid dependency on proprietary CRM systems.

---

# Problem

Traditional onboarding in telecom and customer service is often inefficient.

New employees commonly experience:

* stress during live customer interactions
* uncertainty navigating complex CRM systems
* costly mistakes
* poor confidence
* inconsistent training quality

Current onboarding methods are usually limited to:

* PDFs
* internal documentation
* videos
* shadowing coworkers
* trial-and-error in live systems

This creates operational risk and slows down onboarding.

---

# Solution

This platform acts as a:

* CRM flight simulator
* AI onboarding academy
* workflow training environment

Users can:

* navigate realistic CRM interfaces
* solve simulated customer cases
* practice operational workflows
* receive AI-generated feedback
* improve speed and accuracy over time

# Core Features

## CRM Simulation Environment

A browser-based CRM simulator including:

* customer profiles
* subscriptions
* invoices
* support tickets
* notes
* contract management
* order handling
* cancellation workflows
* retention workflows
* telecom-related operational tasks

The interface is intentionally original and not a clone of existing systems.

## Scenario Engine

* new subscription setup

* billing issues
* upselling opportunities
* retention cases

* identity verification
* customer complaints

Scenarios support:

* randomized data
* hidden issues
* time pressure
* difficulty scaling
